Pricing

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Samsmarana has one free tier and two paid subscriptions. All prices are in Indian Rupees and include any applicable taxes. Payments are processed securely by Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking).

Shloka Cards are free for everyone, on every tier, with or without an account.

जिज्ञासु Jigyasu — The Seeker

Free
  • Shloka of the Day
  • Learning path: Devanagari, Sandhi and Samas
  • Shloka Cards and Sentence Practice
  • Daily quiz, score, badges and streak (with a free account)
  • Read community reflections
  • Guru Ji: up to 5 questions without an account, up to 8 with a free account

साधक Sadhaka — The Practitioner

₹299 / month

or ₹1,999 / year

Everything in Jigyasu, plus:

  • Learning path: Vocabulary, Shabd Roop (all words) and Karaka
  • Guru Ji: up to 15 questions a day, and “Ask Guru Ji” on any shloka
  • Shloka of the Day audio
  • Save shlokas to your bookmarks
  • Post and like reflections in the community

विद्वान् Vidvan — The Learned

₹499 / month

or ₹3,999 / year

Everything in Sadhaka, plus:

  • Learning path: Dhatu Roop (all tenses)
  • Stotrams: all five stotrams line by line, with per-verse audio and full recitation mode

How renewal works

  • Sadhaka and Vidvan are subscriptions. A monthly plan renews automatically every month and a yearly plan every year, charged to the payment method you chose at checkout, until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time; cancelling stops all future renewals. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for how to cancel and what happens to your access.
  • If we ever change a price, the new price applies only from a renewal after we have told you by email.
