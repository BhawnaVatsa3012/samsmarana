Shabd Roop — शब्द रूप
In Sanskrit, a noun changes its ending to show its role. What English does with prepositions — "of", "by", "for" — Sanskrit does by transforming the word itself.
बालक — Boy (Masculine · अकारान्त)
Nouns like राम, देव, छात्र, गुरु all follow this pattern.

The word Karma (कर्म) is itself a neuter noun — a Shabd Roop of the root kar. When the Gita says "Karmanyevadhikaraste" — your right is to karma alone — it uses the 7th Vibhakti. The action is the location you inhabit.

Dhatu Roop — धातु रूप
Dhatu is the root of every verb. From one seed, an entire forest of meanings grows — across time, mood, and person.
The Gita's most quoted verse uses Lot Lakar — "Arise! Fight!" (उत्तिष्ठ, अर्जुन). Commands in Sanskrit are not harsh — they carry the weight of love and duty combined.
व्याकरण
Sandhi — सन्धि
Sandhi means union — the blending of sounds at word boundaries. Sanskrit does not tolerate awkward gaps. Like rivers joining the sea, sounds merge into something seamless.
1. Swar Sandhi — Vowel Union
TypeRuleExampleMeaning
दीर्घa+a=āराम+अयन=रामायणRamayana
गुणa+i=eदेव+इन्द्र=देवेन्द्रKing of Gods
गुणa+u=oगङ्गा+उदक=गङ्गोदकGanga water
यण्i+a=yaइति+आदि=इत्यादिAnd so on
2. Vyanjan Sandhi — Consonant Union
RuleExampleMeaning
t+vowel→dजगत्+ईश=जगदीशLord of Universe
s→ṣ after i/u/rअभि+सेक=अभिषेकCoronation
3. Visarga Sandhi — The Breath
RuleExampleMeaning
aḥ+vowel→o'रामः+अस्ति=रामोऽस्तिRam exists
aḥ+voiced→oबालकः+गच्छति=बालको गच्छतिBoy goes
Namaste is itself a Sandhi — namas + te. "I bow to you." Two words, spoken millions of times daily, that most people don't know are grammatically fused. Sandhi is everywhere once you see it.

Samas — समास
The art of compression. Two words becoming one, carrying the weight of both. Sanskrit says in one word what English takes a sentence to express.
1. Tatpurusha — तत्पुरुष
Second word dominates. First qualifies it.
CompoundSplitMeaning
राजपुत्रःराज्ञः पुत्रःSon of the king
विद्यालयःविद्यायाः आलयःHome of knowledge
देवालयःदेवस्य आलयःTemple
2. Dvandva — द्वन्द्व
Both words are equal — a union of equals.
CompoundSplitMeaning
सुखदुःखेसुखम् च दुःखम् चJoy and sorrow
पितरौमाता च पिता चParents
3. Bahuvrihi — बहुव्रीहि
Neither word is the subject — together they describe a third entity. Sanskrit poetry uses these as divine epithets.
CompoundMeaningRefers to
नीलकण्ठBlue-throated oneShiva
चक्रपाणिChakra in handVishnu
पीताम्बरYellow-robed oneKrishna
4. Avyayibhava — अव्ययीभाव
CompoundMeaning
यथाशक्तिAccording to ability
प्रतिदिनम्Every day
Mahābhārata is a Tatpurusha — mahā + bhārata — "the great tale of the Bharatas." Upanishad is also a compound — upa (near) + ni (down) + shad (to sit) — "sitting near the teacher." Every Sanskrit word you use is a compressed universe of meaning.

Karaka & Vibhakti — कारक
Sanskrit sees every noun as playing a role in the drama of a sentence — and names each role precisely. This is Karaka — the grammar of relationships.
CaseKarakaEnglishExample
1stकर्ता — DoerSubjectरामः पठति
2ndकर्म — Action's objectObjectपुस्तकम् पठति
3rdकरण — InstrumentBy/Withलेखन्या लिखति
4thसम्प्रदान — RecipientFor/Toगुरवे नमः
5thअपादान — SeparationFromग्रामात् आगच्छति
6thसम्बन्ध — RelationOfरामस्य पुस्तकम्
7thअधिकरण — LocationIn/Atविद्यालये पठति
Guruve namah (गुरवे नमः) — the 4th Vibhakti. "I bow to the teacher." Not just any teacher — the one to whom something is given, offered, surrendered. The grammar holds the devotion within it.
लिपि
Devanagari — देवनागरी
The script of the gods. Every letter has one sound — no ambiguity, no exceptions. Once truly learned, it cannot be unlearned.
Vowels — स्वर
Consonants — व्यञ्जन (first 10)
The horizontal line connecting letters — the शिरोरेखा (head line) — is unique to Devanagari. Like the thread of a garland, it holds everything together.
