What English does with prepositions — "of", "by", "for" — Sanskrit does by transforming the word itself. बालक (m) लता (f) फल (n) बालक — Boy (Masculine · अकारान्त) Nouns like राम, देव, छात्र, गुरु all follow this pattern. The word Karma (कर्म) is itself a neuter noun — a Shabd Roop of the root kar. When the Gita says "Karmanyevadhikaraste" — your right is to karma alone — it uses the 7th Vibhakti. The action is the location you inhabit. व्याकरण Dhatu Roop — धातु रूप Dhatu is the root of every verb. From one seed, an entire forest of meanings grows — across time, mood, and person. लट् Present लृट् Future लङ् Past लोट् Command विधि Should The Gita's most quoted verse uses Lot Lakar — "Arise! Fight!" (उत्तिष्ठ, अर्जुन). Commands in Sanskrit are not harsh — they carry the weight of love and duty combined. व्याकरण Sandhi — सन्धि Sandhi means union — the blending of sounds at word boundaries. Sanskrit does not tolerate awkward gaps. Like rivers joining the sea, sounds merge into something seamless. 1. Visarga Sandhi — The Breath RuleExampleMeaning aḥ+vowel→o'रामः+अस्ति=रामोऽस्तिRam exists aḥ+voiced→oबालकः+गच्छति=बालको गच्छतिBoy goes Namaste is itself a Sandhi — namas + te. "I bow to you." Two words, spoken millions of times daily, that most people don't know are grammatically fused. Sandhi is everywhere once you see it. व्याकरण Samas — समास The art of compression. Two words becoming one, carrying the weight of both. Sanskrit says in one word what English takes a sentence to express. 1. Tatpurusha — तत्पुरुष Second word dominates. First qualifies it. CompoundSplitMeaning राजपुत्रःराज्ञः पुत्रःSon of the king विद्यालयःविद्यायाः आलयःHome of knowledge देवालयःदेवस्य आलयःTemple 2. Dvandva — द्वन्द्व Both words are equal — a union of equals. CompoundSplitMeaning सुखदुःखेसुखम् च दुःखम् चJoy and sorrow पितरौमाता च पिता चParents 3. Bahuvrihi — बहुव्रीहि Neither word is the subject — together they describe a third entity. Sanskrit poetry uses these as divine epithets. CompoundMeaningRefers to नीलकण्ठBlue-throated oneShiva चक्रपाणिChakra in handVishnu पीताम्बरYellow-robed oneKrishna 4. Avyayibhava — अव्ययीभाव CompoundMeaning यथाशक्तिAccording to ability प्रतिदिनम्Every day Mahābhārata is a Tatpurusha — mahā + bhārata — "the great tale of the Bharatas." Upanishad is also a compound — upa (near) + ni (down) + shad (to sit) — "sitting near the teacher." Every Sanskrit word you use is a compressed universe of meaning. व्याकरण Karaka & Vibhakti — कारक Sanskrit sees every noun as playing a role in the drama of a sentence — and names each role precisely. This is Karaka — the grammar of relationships. "I bow to the teacher." Not just any teacher — the one to whom something is given, offered, surrendered. The grammar holds the devotion within it. लिपि Devanagari — देवनागरी The script of the gods. Every letter has one sound — no ambiguity, no exceptions. Once truly learned, it cannot be unlearned. Vowels — स्वर Consonants — व्यञ्जन (first 10) The horizontal line connecting letters — the शिरोरेखा (head line) — is unique to Devanagari. Like the thread of a garland, it holds everything together. अभ्यास Vocabulary — शब्द These are not just words. Each one carries centuries of meaning. Some you use daily without knowing their depth.
