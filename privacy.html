Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Samsmarana collects, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025. It is written to be read on its own.

2. Consent

We process your data based on your consent, which you give when you create an account, and for the purposes listed above. Optional items (Google sign-in, profile, reflections, reminders, Vidvan updates, Guru Ji) are used only if you choose to use them. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 5). Withdrawing does not affect processing that already happened, but some features will stop working, and withdrawing consent for your account as a whole means we delete it.

3. Who we share data with

We share data only with these service providers (Data Processors), only for the purpose stated, and under their data-protection terms:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in provider. Stores your account and everything in section 1.
  • Vercel: hosts the app and runs our server code. Processes requests, and keeps short-lived technical logs.
  • Google (Gemini API): generates Guru Ji’s answers. Each time you ask a question, your conversation so far is sent to Google. Samsmarana does not send your name, email, bio or any other account details with these questions. Guru Ji conversations are not saved by Samsmarana; they disappear when you leave or reload the page. Guru Ji uses Google’s free Gemini API tier, under which Google may use your questions and Guru Ji’s answers, including review by people, to improve its products. Please do not share personal details — such as your name, phone number, address, health or financial information — in your questions to Guru Ji.
  • Razorpay: processes payments. Receives your email, the plan you chose and your Samsmarana user ID, and collects your payment details directly.
  • Google (Sign-in): only if you choose “Continue with Google”.
  • Your browser’s push service (e.g. Google, Apple, Mozilla): delivers the daily reminder if you turn it on.

Some of these providers may store data outside India. We may also disclose data where required by law.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, progress, bookmarks and reflections: while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account (see section 5), we delete this data, except anything we must keep by law.
  • Payment records held by Razorpay are kept by Razorpay as required by Indian financial regulations.
  • Push subscriptions: until you turn reminders off, or your browser reports them as expired.
  • Guru Ji conversations: not stored by us.

5. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access: get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with;
  • Correction and completion: fix or update inaccurate data;
  • Erasure: have your account and data deleted;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it;
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to;
  • Grievance redressal: complain to us and get a response.

How to exercise them

  • In the app: edit your name and bio under Personal Details, delete your own reflections and bookmarks, and turn the daily reminder off.
  • For everything else (a copy of your data, account deletion, nominating someone, withdrawing consent), email vatsa.bhawna@gmail.com or our Grievance Officer from the email address on your account. There is no in-app deletion button yet; email is the way to do it. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

7. Children

Samsmarana is meant for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly process personal data of anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. If we learn that such an account exists, we will delete it. If you believe a child has signed up, write to vatsa.bhawna@gmail.com.

8. Security

  • All traffic between your device and Samsmarana is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored hashed by Supabase, not in readable form.
  • Your data is protected by access rules so users can see only their own private data. Only our server code, using keys kept out of the app, can make account-level changes.
  • We never handle card or UPI details.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Rules require.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update the “Last updated” date when this policy changes, and tell you by email or in the app before any significant change takes effect.
