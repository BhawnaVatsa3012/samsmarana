How to request a refund

  1. Email vatsa.bhawna@gmail.com from the email address on your Samsmarana account.
  2. Include the date and amount of the payment and, if you have it, the Razorpay payment ID from your receipt email.
  3. We will confirm by email whether the refund is approved, and when it has been issued.

Cancelling your subscription

Sadhaka and Vidvan subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. To cancel:

  1. Email vatsa.bhawna@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “Cancel subscription”.
  2. We will cancel the subscription with Razorpay and confirm by email. No further renewal will be charged after cancellation.

Failed or duplicate payments

If money was deducted but your plan did not activate, or you were charged twice, email us with the payment details. We will either activate your plan or refund the payment in full.
