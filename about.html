About

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Samsmarana (संस्मरण, “remembrance”) is a web app for adults who studied Sanskrit in school, usually for a CBSE board exam, and want to finally understand it.

Most of us learned to memorise शब्द रूप tables for marks. Samsmarana starts from what you half-remember and connects it to the shlokas, stotrams and ideas that made the language worth learning in the first place.

Who it is for

Working adults, parents and lifelong learners who once read these words in school and are now ready to understand them. No prior fluency is needed; if you can recognise a little Devanagari, you can start.

The 8-step learning path

  1. Devanagari — the script: vowels and all 33 consonants.
  2. Sandhi — how sounds join when words meet.
  3. Samas — how words combine into compounds.
  4. Vocabulary — key Sanskrit words, each anchored in a real shloka.
  5. Shabd Roop — how nouns change form to show their role.
  6. Dhatu Roop — how verbs change across tense, person and number.
  7. Karaka — the logic of who does what to whom, and the Vibhaktis that express it.
  8. Stotrams — full stotrams, line by line, with meaning and recitation audio.

Alongside the path: Shloka of the Day, a daily quiz, Shloka Cards, sentence practice, a community space for reflections, and Guru Ji, an AI tutor for questions along the way.

A note from the founder

I’m Bhawna Mishra. Like many of you, I studied Sanskrit in school, scored my marks, and then put it away for years. When I came back to it as an adult, I found that the shlokas I had once memorised had something to say about the life I was actually living. I built Samsmarana to make that return easier for others. The stotram recitations in the app are recorded in my own voice.
